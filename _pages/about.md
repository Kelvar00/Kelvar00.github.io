## About Me
I'm an undergraduate student at Zhejiang University, currently pursuing a degree in Artificial Intelligence (Turing Class).

My research interests include 3D/4D reconstruction, vision, rendering, and deep learning.

I will continue to pursue a Master's degree at the State Key Lab of CAD&CG, Zhejiang University, under the supervision of Prof. Weiwei Xu. I was also honored to conduct summer research under the guidance of Prof. Evan Peng and Dr. Xiaoyang Bai at WeLight Lab, HKU.
